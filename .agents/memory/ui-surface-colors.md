---
name: White interface surfaces
description: Keep feature-page backgrounds and cards consistent with the app's usual white interface.
---

Use white for primary page panels, cards, tables, and form controls; use neutral light-gray borders and toolbar backgrounds. Keep green, gold, red, and other tints for actions, status badges, and clearly semantic alerts rather than as broad page surfaces.

**Why:** the user noted that Avances Exportateurs and Sacherie looked unusually cream-colored compared with the rest of the app.

**How to apply:** when restyling or adding pages in this app, match the white surfaces used by the other operational pages and reserve color tints for small, meaningful accents.
